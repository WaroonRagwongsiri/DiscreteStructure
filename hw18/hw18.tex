\section{Who is the killer?}
}
Alice, Alice's husband, their son, their daughter, and Alice's brother were involved in a murder.
One of the five killed one of the other four. The following facts refer to the five people mentioned:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item A man and a woman were together in a bar at the time of the murder.
	\item The victim and the killer were together on a beach at the time of the murder.
	\item One of Alice's two children was alone at the time of the murder.
	\item Alice and her husband were not together at the time of the murder.
	\item The victim's twin was not the killer.
	\item The killer was younger than the victim.
\end{enumerate}

Which one of the five was the victim? Who is the killer?

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Alice husband, Alice brother is in the beach
	\item Alice son, Alice is in the bar
	\item Alice daughter is alone

	\item Alice husband is killer
	\item Alice brother is victim
\end{enumerate}
\paragraph{This assumtion fit all the given facts}
\therefore{
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item Alice husband, Alice brother is in the beach
		\item Alice son, Alice is in the bar
		\item Alice daughter is alone

		\item Alice husband is killer
		\item Alice brother is victim
	\end{enumerate}
}
